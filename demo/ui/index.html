        How it works · 4 steps

        Step 1

        You paste a ticket

        Helpdesk tickets and emails are full of personal details: names, Aadhaar and PAN numbers, bank accounts, salaries, dates of birth.

        Step 2

        Personal details become placeholders

        Before anything leaves, the gateway swaps each detail for a code. Bank, Aadhaar and PAN numbers are withheld completely. Salary maths is done inside the company.

        Step 3

        The AI only sees the codes

        A safety check runs on the outgoing message first. If something is missing or can’t be checked, the request is stopped or kept with an in-company model.

        Step 4

        Real names come back to you

        The codes in the AI’s answer are turned back into the real names, inside the company. You read a normal reply. The AI never had the details.
